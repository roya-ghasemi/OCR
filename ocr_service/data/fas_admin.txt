# Fixed vocabulary of Persian administrative letters (E27).
#
# Tesseract's own Persian word list is a general one and is missing words that are on
# almost every letter in this corpus. A missing word is not merely unrepaired: it looks
# like a non-word, so the dot repair "corrects" it into something else. «بسمه» is not in
# fas_words.txt, «تسمه» is, and the opening formula of the brief's image 1 was being
# rewritten into it at full confidence.
#
# These are therefore protections first and candidates second. Every entry is a word that
# is printed on administrative letters as a matter of form, not a word chosen to make one
# document score better.
